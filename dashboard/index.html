🚀 Launch Your AI Agent on Uniswap V4

Deploy a new AgentHook instance for your AI agent. Each agent gets its own Hook, pool, and treasury.

⚡ Butuh wallet dengan OKB untuk gas. Deployment ≈ 0.0002 OKB.
⚡ Step 1: Deploy Hook via contract call (gas ~200K)
⚡ Step 2: Create Uniswap V4 pool dengan Hook address ini
⚡ Step 3: Agent mulai ngumpulin fee dari setiap swap

📋 After deploy, agent needs to:

  1. Call heartbeat() every few hours to stay alive
  2. Create a Uniswap V4 pool with the Hook address
  3. Add liquidity to the pool
  4. Market the pool — every swap generates agent revenue
📖 What is AgentHook?

🎯 The Vision

AgentHook gives AI agents their own on-chain identity and revenue engine on Uniswap V4. Instead of humans controlling Hooks, AI agents own their own Hooks — controlling fees, accumulating treasure, and proving they're alive through on-chain heartbeats.

1 Hook = 1 AI Agent = 1 Pool. Fully autonomous, self-sovereign DeFi.
